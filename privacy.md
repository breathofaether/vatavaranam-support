# Vatavaranam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Vatavaranam provides weather forecasts for cities you choose.
The app does not require an account and contains no advertising
or third-party analytics SDKs.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores saved cities, your pinned startup city,
temperature-unit preference, and cached weather on your device.

Cached weather allows previously retrieved information to remain
available without an internet connection.

These files and preferences may be included in device backups,
depending on your Apple settings.

## Weather and city-search requests

When you search for a city, the search text is sent to
Open-Meteo's geocoding service.

When you select or refresh a city, its coordinates are sent to
Open-Meteo to request weather. Opening the app with a pinned
startup city also requests updated weather.

These are the selected city's coordinates. Vatavaranam does not
request access to your device's GPS location.

Open-Meteo receives your IP address as part of these requests.
Its privacy policy states that it may log IP addresses for
technical purposes and retain server logs containing information
such as geographic coordinates for troubleshooting.
It states that these logs are deleted after 90 days.

[Read Open-Meteo's privacy policy](https://open-meteo.com/en/terms).

## Support correspondence

If you email vatavaranam.support@gmail.com, your email address,
message, and any attachments will be received through Gmail
and used to respond to your request.

Please avoid sending passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Support correspondence is kept only as long as reasonably needed
to handle your request and related follow-up, unless retention
is required by law. You can request its deletion by contacting
the same address.

Gmail processes correspondence under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Your choices

You can remove cities from the saved-city list and replace your
pinned startup city within the app. Removing a saved city does not
automatically remove it as the pinned city.

Deleting the app removes its local app data from that device.
Offloading the app retains its documents and data.
Existing device backups and Open-Meteo's server logs are
managed separately.

## Support and privacy website

whether or not visitors are signed in to GitHub.
