# Vatavaranam — Support

Weather for the cities you choose.

## Contact

Need help? Email [vatavaranam.support@gmail.com](mailto:vatavaranam.support@gmail.com).

When reporting a problem, include:

- Your iPhone model and iOS version.
- The app version.
- What you were doing and what happened.
- The city and region involved, if relevant.
- A screenshot, if helpful, with personal information hidden.

Never send passwords or account recovery codes.

## Why does refreshing show the same weather?

Refresh requests the latest available data from Open-Meteo.
Consecutive requests may return the same values.
Check the weather-data timestamp shown in the app.

## Why does the app show saved weather?

Previously fetched weather can be displayed from your device's cache.
The saved-weather caption identifies cached information.
An internet connection is needed to request updated forecasts.

## How do I change the startup city?

Search for and select a city, then tap its pin button.
You can return to it through the Pinned city section in Saved Cities.

Removing a saved city does not automatically unpin it.
Pin another city to change the startup selection.

## Can I change temperature units?

Open the gear menu and use the “Use °F” toggle.

## Weather information

Weather is provided by [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/),
with location data from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/).

Forecasts may differ between providers and may be unavailable or
out of date. For severe-weather warnings and safety decisions,
consult your official local weather authority.
